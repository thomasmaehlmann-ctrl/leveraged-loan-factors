Direct lending returns from public data

The index follows the loans that registered funds report quarter by quarter in Form N-PORT: corporate loans without a public identifier, valued at fair value level 3. Returns combine interest income with the change in fund valuations, including losses on loans that default. The quarterly returns track the Cliffwater Direct Lending Index, which is built from business development companies, with a correlation of 0.93.

Credit health barometer

Share of loans that no longer pay interest (non-accrual) and share of loans valued below 90, weighted by estimated cost. Cliffwater, the largest manager with about three quarters of the market value, reports very few such loans; without Cliffwater, the levels are close to those reported for business development companies.

Returns in % per quarter, gross of fees and unlevered. Income: coupon accrual; price: change in fund valuations, including realized losses. More series and the full barometer on the analysis page.

How to read the index. Returns reflect fund valuations, not transaction prices. They react to market moves with less than half the amplitude of traded loans (Dimson beta on the leveraged loan market: 0.43), so volatility and drawdowns appear lower than they would be at market prices.
