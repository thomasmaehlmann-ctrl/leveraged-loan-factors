Open data for corporate loans

Return series for US leveraged loans and for direct lending in Europe and the US, built entirely from two public SEC sources: the holdings of US registered funds (Form N-PORT) and the schedules of investments of business development companies (BDCs). Every number can be reproduced from public filings with open code.

Open US Leveraged Loan Factors

Monthly returns of nine factors for US syndicated leveraged loans in 48 specifications, and a market index that tracks the Morningstar LSTA US Leveraged Loan Index with a correlation of 0.995.

Why public data

Prices of leveraged loans and private loans come from commercial vendors whose licenses prohibit sharing them, and the valuations behind established private credit benchmarks cannot be fully inspected. Research and benchmarks in these markets are therefore hard to replicate. Two kinds of SEC filings are public and report loan holdings at fair value; aggregated across funds, they cover a large part of both markets.
