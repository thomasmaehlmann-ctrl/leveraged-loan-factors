European direct lending from public data

US business development companies hold a large and growing book of European loans: about USD 20bn in the latest quarter, held by 50 BDCs, of which 55% in euro and 40% in sterling. OpenDL Europe follows these loans quarter by quarter. Compared with the S&P Lincoln European Senior Debt Index, its first-lien sub-index has a correlation of 0.67 and differs by –0.80 percentage points per year.

Returns in % per quarter in loan currency, gross of fees and unlevered. More series, sub-indices for first-lien, euro and sterling loans and the full barometer on the analysis page.

What the index covers. European direct lending held by the BDCs of US and global managers, such as Blackstone, Apollo, HPS, Goldman Sachs, Blue Owl and Oaktree. European lenders that do not file with the SEC are not included. Returns reflect fund valuations, not transaction prices. See the methodology.
