About

loanfactors.org is maintained by Thomas Mählmann, Professor at the Department of Finance and Banking of the Catholic University of Eichstätt-Ingolstadt.

The site provides open return data for two segments of the corporate loan market that lack public prices: broadly syndicated leveraged loans and direct lending. The data are built from two public SEC sources: the holdings that US registered funds report in Form N-PORT, and the schedules of investments that business development companies (BDCs) file in machine-readable form. All data come with open code, so that every number can be reproduced.

  • Leveraged Loan Factors continues a research program on systematic investing in leveraged loans. Earlier work, based on commercial dealer quotes, showed that value, momentum and related factor themes are priced in the loan market. The database makes the core factor returns available to everyone, using public data only.
  • The Open Direct Lending Indices extend this approach to private credit: OpenDL Europe measures European direct lending from the holdings of BDCs, OpenDL US BDC US direct lending from the same filings, and OpenDL US US direct lending from the holdings of interval and other registered closed-end funds, each with a barometer of credit health.
