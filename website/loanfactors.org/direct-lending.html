Why open indices

Direct loans are not traded. Their returns are measured from the valuations that lenders report, and the established benchmarks rest on data that cannot be fully inspected. The OpenDL indices use only public filings: the holdings that US registered funds (interval and other closed-end funds) report in Form N-PORT for OpenDL US, and the machine-readable schedules of investments of business development companies (BDCs) for OpenDL Europe. Every number can be reproduced with the published code.

How to read the indices. Returns reflect fund valuations, not transaction prices, and are smoother than the returns of traded loans. OpenDL Europe covers European direct lending held by the BDCs of US and global managers; European lenders that do not file with the SEC are not included. See the methodology.
