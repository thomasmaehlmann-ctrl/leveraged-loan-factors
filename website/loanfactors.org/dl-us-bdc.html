The broad view of US direct lending

BDCs hold the largest part of US direct lending that is reported loan by loan: about USD 500bn in the latest quarter, held by 150 BDCs. Built from the same filings as the Cliffwater Direct Lending Index (CDLI), OpenDL US BDC tracks it with a quarterly correlation of 0.97 and nearly identical price returns (– against – per year); its income is lower because it uses contractual coupons, without fees and the accretion of discounts.

Two US indices. OpenDL US BDC measures the US direct loans of BDCs; OpenDL US measures those of interval funds and other registered closed-end funds, from Form N-PORT. The same manager values loans to the same borrower alike in both vehicles; different managers disagree on troubled loans. Comparing the two indices shows how much a direct lending index depends on whose valuations it aggregates. See the methodology.
